# Messe Wien Survivor

Create a premium, ultra-minimalist landing page for a WU Vienna BBE preparatory platform called "Messe Wien Survivor". 

Color Palette: Strict luxury dark mode. Background: solid dark graphite (#121212). Typography: crisp white and muted slate gray text. Accents: deep corporate burgundy (#7A0026) for primary elements and subtle neon white glows. 



The page must contain the following sections in a seamless layout:



1. HERO SECTION:

- Left side: High-contrast bold typography. Main title: "Messe Wien 2026 In-Person Exam Cracked." Subtitle: "The strict risk-management simulator built by a Top-240 survivor. Master the 42-second time crunch and the brutal Partial-Credit marking system." 

- Add two minimalist CTA buttons: A solid burgundy button "Access the Simulator Engine" and a secondary white ghost button "Parents' Financial Guide (Download PDF)".

- Right side: A modern, sleek video player placeholder with a dark semi-transparent glassmorphic frame. Inside the player, show a clean centered text placeholder saying: "Watch Intro Video: Passing the Exam from Rimini Beach".



2. FINANCIAL PRESTIGE & ROI BLOCK (The Alternative Cost):

- Create a strict 2-column comparison matrix titled "The Economics of Admission: Expense vs. Investment".

- Left Column: Title "The $20,000/Year Trap". Text: "Attending a private university or a lower-tier business school means burning over $20,000 annually in tuition. Over 3 years, that is a $60,000 family liability with zero historical prestige."

- Right Column: Title "The WU Vienna Arbitrage". Text: "WU Vienna is a world-class, triple-accredited elite business school with tuition fees of virtually €0. Passing this exam is a massive high-ROI financial pivot."

- Add a bottom alert box with a thin burgundy border stating: "By investing today, you block tens of thousands in future tuition debts. This is a pure financial hedge."



3. FIELD REPORTS (Student Reviews):

- A clean 3-column borderless grid titled "Field Reports from Messe Wien".

- Inside the cards, display borderless text reviews from students using real exam slang (e.g., "Saved by the 2-3 answers discipline", "Avoided the marking system trap", "Furmman's textbook traps bypassed").

- Each card must have a small bold badge: "Official Score: 84.0%", "Official Score: 79.5%", etc.



Ensure the entire page has generous spacing, flawless responsive layout, and an elite corporate aesthetic using Tailwind CSS. No cheesy icons or star ratings. Keep it sharp and executive.
