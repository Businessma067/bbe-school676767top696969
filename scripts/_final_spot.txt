=== DEMO MATH 8.H01 A len=789

Read the daytime tariff from the stem: a fixed base of EUR $12$ plus EUR $0.8$ per kilometre. Write the base alone:

$$
0.8\cdot d
$$

$$
12+0.8d
$$

So the daytime bill is

$$
C_{\mathrm{day}}(d)=12+0.8d
$$

Spot-check at $d=10$:

$$
0.8\times 10=8\qquad 12+8=20
$$

$$
C_{\mathrm{day}}(10)=20
$$

$$
0.8\times 20
$$

$$
0.8\times 10=8\qquad 8+8=16
$$

$$

$$

$$
C_{\mathrm{day}}(20)=28
$$

$$
0.8\times 5=4\qquad 12+4=16
$$

$$
C_{\mathrm{day}}(5)=16

$$
0.8\times 25
$$

$$
0.8\times 5=4\qquad 16+4=20

$$
12+20=32\qquad C_{\mathrm{day}}(25)=32
$$

Each check equals $12+0.8d$ euros. The claimed daytime formula matches the tariff exactly.

So the statement is True.
