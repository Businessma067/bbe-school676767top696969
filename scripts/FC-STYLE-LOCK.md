# Full Course style lock (mandatory)

## Context pattern (required)
One or two clauses, academic, ending with:
`Evaluate the following economic assertions:`

Examples:
- `Analyze how businesses classify human resources among the factors of production used to create goods and services. Evaluate the following economic assertions:`
- `Consider a wine-growing business that uses hillside vineyards, seasonal pickers, leased bottling equipment, and specialist fermentation know-how. Evaluate the following economic assertions:`
- `Review the definition of gross domestic product as a measure of overall economic activity within a country. Evaluate the following economic assertions:`

FORBIDDEN context styles:
- `Evaluate claims about X:`
- `A Styrian winemaker hires…. Evaluate:`
- Starting with only `Evaluate:` / `Assess:` / short stubs

## Titles
Academic Title Case, like Full Course: `Labour as a Factor of Production`, not `Labour as human resources`.

## Statements
- One full formal sentence each, ~15–35 words when natural.
- Book concepts ONLY for that subsection; use book wording (extraction of raw materials from the earth; transform raw materials into goods; service industry; human resources; natural resources; machinery, plant, vehicles, financial resources; etc.).
- Subtle traps; no neon-only FALSE via automatically/never/zero/always as the sole signal.
- NO “the book”, “according to the book”, “(alt…)”, “Fuhrmann notes”.

## Explanations
Teacher voice, same depth as math tactical explanations:
- No `TRUE —` / `FALSE —` leads and no em dashes.
- Calm tutor prose: why the claim holds or fails, every needed step for numbers/ratios.
- Plain closing sentence: `The statement is true.` / `The statement is false.`
- Practice UI already prints `**A.** → True/False` above the body; do not repeat that stamp.

## Mix
~25% lived scenes woven into Consider/Analyze context; ~75% theory Analyze/Review stems.

## Uniqueness
Every statement globally unique. No near-duplicates within a case (no synonym twins). Distinct explanations within a case.
