=== EXPL 0 A ===
**A.** → True

This statement asks for the total number of possible 4-club exhibition fields. This is equivalent to finding the total number of ways to choose $4$ clubs from the $16$ available clubs, without regard to order. This is a combination problem, represented by $\binom{16}{4}$.

$$
\binom{16}{4} = \dfrac{16!}{4!(16-4)!}
$$

$$
\dfrac{16!}{4!(12)!} = \dfrac{16 \cdot 15 \cdot 14 \cdot 13}{1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 4}
$$

$$
16 \times 15 = 240
$$

$$
240 \times 14 = 3360
$$

$$
3360 \times 13 = 43680
$$

$$
\dfrac{43680}{24} = 1820
$$

$$
\binom{16}{4} = 1820
$$

We find that there are $1,820$ possible combinations of $4$ clubs. The claim states that the total number of possible 4-club exhibition fields is $1,820$.

So the statement is True.


=== EXPL 1 B ===
**B.** → True

This statement asks for the probability that the exhibition contains exactly $2$ seeded clubs. This means we need to select $2$ seeded clubs from the $6$ available seeded clubs and $2$ unseeded clubs from the $10$ available unseeded clubs. The probability is calculated as the ratio of the number of favorable outcomes to the total number of possible outcomes.

The number of ways to choose exactly $2$ seeded clubs and $2$ unseeded clubs is $\binom{6}{2} \binom{10}{2}$. From the overview, this value is $675$.
The total number of ways to choose $4$ clubs from $16$ is $\binom{16}{4}$. From the overview, this value is $1,820$.

So, the probability $P(k=2)$ is:

$$
\binom{16}{4} = \dfrac{16!}{4!(16-4)!}
$$

$$
\dfrac{16!}{4!(12)!} = \dfrac{16 \cdot 15 \cdot 14 \cdot 13}{1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 4}
$$

$$
16 \times 15 = 240
$$

$$
240 \times 14 = 3360
$$

$$
3360 \times 13 = 43680
$$

$$
\dfrac{43680}{24} = 1820
$$

$$
\binom{16}{4} = 1820
$$

$$
P(k = 2) = \frac{\binom{6}{2} \binom{10}{2}}{\binom{16}{4}} = \frac{675}{1,820}
$$

$$
\approx 0.370879
$$

As a percentage, this is approximately $37.09\%$. The claim states that this probability is greater than $35\%$.
