The probability that no friend receives their own gift means we are looking for the probability of $0$ matches, which is $P(0 \text{ matches})$. The number of such arrangements is given by the derangement number $D_n$. For $n=6$, we need $D_6$. From the overview, we calculated $D_6 = 265$. The total number of ways to redistribute the gifts is $6! = 720$.

The probability is the ratio of derangements to total permutations:

$$
P(0 \text{ matches}) = \frac{D_6}{6!}
$$

$$
P(0 \text{ matches}) = \frac{265}{720}
$$

$$
P(0 \text{ matches}) \approx 0.3680555
$$

Expressed as a percentage, this is approximately $36.80555\%$. The claim states that this probability is greater than $36.8\%$.

So the statement is True.
