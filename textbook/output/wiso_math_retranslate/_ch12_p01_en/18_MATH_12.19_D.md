Since each of these units is unique (i.e., there are no repeated units among these six), the number of distinct arrangements is simply $6!$.

$$
\text{Arrangements with SSSS and EE adjacent} = 6!

$$
P(\text{SSSS adjacent and EE adjacent}) = \frac{720}{75,600}

P(\text{SSSS adjacent and EE adjacent}) = \frac{1}{105}
