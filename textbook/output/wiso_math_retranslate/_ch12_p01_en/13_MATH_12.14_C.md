$$
\binom{5}{4} \times \binom{8}{1}
$$

$$
\binom{5}{4} = \dfrac{5!}{4!(5-4)!}
$$

$$
\binom{5}{4} = 5
$$

$$
\binom{8}{1} = \dfrac{8!}{1!(8-1)!}
$$

$$
\binom{8}{1} = 8 = 5 \times 8
$$

$$
5 \times 8 = 40
$$

\binom{5}{5} = 1

$$
\binom{8}{0} = 1 = 1 \times 1

1 \times 1 = 1
