Since all these 5 items are distinct (B, A, the 'LL' block, the 'OO' block, and N), the number of arrangements is simply the factorial of the number of items.

$$
N_{\text{L and O adjacent}} = 5!
$$

$$
N_{\text{L and O adjacent}} = 120
$$

The total number of distinct arrangements, $N_{\text{total}}$, is $1260$. The probability is the ratio of these favorable arrangements to the total arrangements.

$$
P(\text{L and O adjacent}) = \frac{N_{\text{L and O adjacent}}}{N_{\text{total}}}
$$

$$
P(\text{L and O adjacent}) = \frac{120}{1260}
$$

P(\text{L and O adjacent}) = \frac{12}{126}
