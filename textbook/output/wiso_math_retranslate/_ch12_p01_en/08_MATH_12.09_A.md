This statement asks for the total number of distinct circular arrangements for 8 people. When arranging $n$ distinct items in a circle where rotations are considered identical, the formula for the number of arrangements is $(n-1)!$. This effectively fixes one person's position and then arranges the remaining $n-1$ people linearly relative to the fixed person.

For $n=8$ people, the total number of distinct circular arrangements is:

$$
(8-1)! = 7!
$$

$$
7! = 7 \cdot 6 \cdot 5 \cdot 4 \cdot 3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1
$$

$$
7 \times 6 = 42
$$

$$
42 \times 5 = 210
$$

$$
210 \times 4 = 840
$$

$$
840 \times 3 = 2520
$$

$$
2520 \times 2 = 5040
$$

$$
5040 \times 1 = 5040
$$

$$
7! = 5040
$$

This calculation shows that there are $5040$ distinct circular arrangements. The claim states that the total number of distinct circular arrangements is $5040$.

So the statement is True.
