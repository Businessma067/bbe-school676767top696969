This statement concerns the probability of rolling no 6 at all in 4 rolls of a single die. This means that each of the 4 independent rolls must result in an outcome other than a 6. The probability of not rolling a 6 on a single roll is $\frac{5}{6}$. Since the rolls are independent, we multiply the probabilities for each roll.

$$
P(\text{no 6 at all in 4 rolls}) = \left(\frac{5}{6}\right)^4
$$

$$
P(\text{no 6 at all in 4 rolls}) = \frac{625}{1296}
$$

$$
P(\text{no 6 at all in 4 rolls}) \approx 0.482253
$$

When expressed as a percentage, this is approximately $48.23\%$. The claim states that this probability is less than $45\%$.

So the statement is False.
