This statement asks to consider a hypothetical scenario where all 7 letters were distinct, meaning there are no repeated letters. In this case, the total number of arrangements would be the number of permutations of 7 distinct items. This is given by $7!$.

$$
N_{\text{all distinct}} = 7!
$$

$$
N_{\text{all distinct}} = 5040
$$

This means there would be $5040$ total arrangements if all letters were distinct. The statement claims that this number would be less than $2,520$.

So the statement is False.
