This statement asks for the total number of distinct 5-card hands that can be dealt from a standard 52-card deck. This is a combination problem, as the order of the cards within a hand does not matter. The number of ways to choose 5 cards from 52 is given by the binomial coefficient $\binom{52}{5}$.

The calculation for the total number of hands, $N_{\text{total}}$, is provided in the solution overview:

$$
N_{\text{total}} = \binom{52}{5}
$$

$$
\binom{52}{5} = \dfrac{52!}{5!(52-5)!}
$$

$$
\dfrac{52!}{5!(47)!} = \dfrac{52 \cdot 51 \cdot 50 \cdot 49 \cdot 48}{1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 4 \cdot 5}
$$

$$
52 \cdot 51 = 2652
$$

$$
2652 \cdot 50 = 132600
$$

$$
132600 \cdot 49 = 6497400
$$

$$
6497400 \cdot 48 = 311875200
$$

$$
\dfrac{311875200}{120} = 2598960
$$

$$
\binom{52}{5} = 2598960
$$

$$
N_{\text{total}} = 2,598,960
$$

This value exactly matches the number given in the statement.

So the statement is True.
