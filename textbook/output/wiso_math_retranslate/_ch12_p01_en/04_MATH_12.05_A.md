The claim asks for the total number of possible outcomes when 5 distinguishable six-sided dice are rolled. This is a direct application of the multiplication principle. Each of the 5 dice has 6 independent outcomes. The number of possible outcomes is $6$ raised to the power of the number of dice.

The calculation for the total number of outcomes, $\Omega$, is:

$$
\Omega = 6 \times 6 \times 6 \times 6 \times 6
$$

$$
6 \times 6 = 36
$$

$$
36 \times 6 = 216
$$

$$
216 \times 6 = 1296
$$

$$
1296 \times 6 = 7776
$$

$$
\Omega = 6^5
$$

$$
\Omega = 7776
$$

This result matches the number given in the statement.

So the statement is True.
