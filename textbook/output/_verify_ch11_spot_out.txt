    [1] The infinite-sum formula a/(1-k) applies the same way regardless of the sign of k, as long as |k|<1. Substituting a=4,000 and k=-0.5 gives 4,000/(1-(-0.5)) = 4,000/1.5 ≈ $2,666.67.

    [4] With k=-1, each new term exactly cancels the one before it: starting from a, the running total becomes a, then 0, then a, then 0, and so on forever, oscillating between those two values without ever settling on one number.
