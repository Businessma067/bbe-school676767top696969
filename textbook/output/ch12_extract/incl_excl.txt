Section  2  —  Answer  Key  &  Explanations 
Each  answer  below  computes  the  union  first,  then  breaks  it  down  further  into  the  exactly-one,  exactly-two,  and  (where  needed)  exactly-three  regions  using  the  formulas  in  the  box  below.  As  a  sanity  check,  exactly-one  +  exactly-two  +  exactly-three  +  none  should  always  total  100%.   
  Question  6  (3  True,  2  False)  Step  1  —  Organize  the  given  values.  P(SQLi)  =  35%,  P(XSS)  =  28%,  P(Misconfig)  =  40%.  P(SQLi ∩ XSS)  =  12%,  P(SQLi ∩ Misconfig)  =  15%,  P(XSS ∩ Misconfig)  =  10%.  P(all  three)  =  5%.  Union  =  (35+28+40)  −  (12+15+10)  +  5  =  103  −  37  +  5  =  71%.  Sum  of  singles  =  103%.  Sum  of  pairwise  =  37%.
