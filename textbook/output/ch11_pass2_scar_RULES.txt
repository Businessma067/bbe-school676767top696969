## PRIORITY FIXES (search every field)

1. Stray `$` glued to prose (VERY IMPORTANT — real UI breaks):
   BAD:  `\$54,000 at the end of Year 1$ and $\$88,000 at the end of Year 2`
   GOOD: `\$54,000 at the end of Year 1 and \$88,000 at the end of Year 2`
   Hunt for: letters/words then `$` then space/`and`, or `$\$digits` followed by English.

2. Prefer algebra without a currency glyph when the number is just a coefficient:
   BAD:  `$\$40{,}000/(1.05)^{2}$`
   GOOD: `$40{,}000/(1.05)^{2}$`
   Keep `\$` in prose money: `approximately \$36,281.18`
   Keep `$a = \$8{,}000$` when you intentionally show a money symbol next to a variable.

3. Never escape the opening math delimiter:
   BAD: `\$3r = \ln(...)$`
   GOOD: `$3r = \ln(...)$`

4. English not inside `$...$`. Split comparisons with prose "and".

5. Flat powers → `^{...}`. Smash `•` bullets → newlines.

6. Do not change answer_key / numeric truth / other subsections.
